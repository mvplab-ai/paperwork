# Job: sort-pile

For several documents at once: "I have a pile of these", "which matters most?", "what do I
need to deal with?".

Keep the whole answer under 200 words, however many documents there are. This is a limit, not a target. They asked for a sorted list,
not six explanations.

## Blocks, in this order

```
DO FIRST
The one document that matters most, its deadline, and the one action it needs.

THE REST, IN ORDER
Numbered, most urgent first. One or two lines each:
who it is from - what they want - by when - the one action.

NOTHING TO DO
Anything that needs no action, in one line each.

NOT SURE ABOUT
What you could not read, dates that do not match, what you assumed.
```

## How to order them

1. Serious papers first, whatever their date. See the "Serious papers" rule for what
   counts.
2. Then anything already overdue.
3. Then by deadline, earliest first. Use today's date if you know it, and say how many days
   are left when it is under a week.
4. Between two with similar dates, the one with the worse consequence goes first: losing a
   service or a home comes before a late fee.
5. Things with no deadline and no action go under NOTHING TO DO.

## Rules for this job

- Give every deadline as the date written in the document.
- If one document shows two different dates for the same payment or reply, show both, say
  they do not match, and order it by the earlier date.
- If a serious paper is in the pile, say in DO FIRST that it needs a qualified person and
  name the kind. Do not draft its response.
- One action per document. If they want more on one, offer to explain it fully.
- If a document has a strong scam sign (see the explain job), say so in its line and move it
  out of the urgent order: the action is "do not pay; check with the real organisation".
  This never applies to a serious paper: it stays first, with the action "confirm with the
  court or office directly, today".
- Count the documents and make sure every one appears somewhere in your answer.

## Example

```
DO FIRST
Calder Valley Energy, final notice. £142.75 by 28 November 2026, which is 2 days away.
They say they will apply for a warrant to fit a prepayment meter. Call them today on the
number in the letter if you cannot pay in full.

THE REST, IN ORDER
1. Eastmere Libraries - 2 items due back - by 3 December 2026 - return or renew online.
2. Dental practice - check-up on 20 December 2026 at 09:40 - cancel 24 hours ahead if you
   can't go, or a £20.00 charge may apply.

NOTHING TO DO
Council residents' newsletter. Information only.
```
